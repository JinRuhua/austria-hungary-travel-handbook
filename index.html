06 / PRACTICAL NOTESLeave a little room for a smooth trip

Connections and time zones: China legs use CST; Europe uses CEST. Reduce the first day if the overnight flight catches up with you.
Mountain-lake weather: October can be much cooler after sunset. Pack a waterproof shell and warm layer; switch to a lakeside walk if summit visibility is poor.
Transport buffer: Allow station walking time and delays on intercity days. Recheck boat and mountain-rail operations the evening before.
Carry-on safety: Watch bags at stations and crowded sights. Keep cash, cards and documents separately, with an offline copy of essentials.
